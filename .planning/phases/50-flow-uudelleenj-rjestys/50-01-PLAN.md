<objective>
Migrate in-flight onboarding drafts to the new step numbering and tighten the save-step
route's bounds check so the stored `current_step` can never exceed the new 5-step wizard range.

This is the database/backend half of Phase 50. It must ship together with (and ahead of)
the app-code reorder in 50-02 so that resuming drafts land on the correct step after the
wizard renumbers StepMediat from old step 2 to new step 1.

Purpose: Implement FLOW-04 — drafts created under the old 6-position step order (StepPaikka=1,
StepMediat=2 ... StepEsikatselu=6) resume correctly after StepPaikka is pulled out of the
numbered wizard, shifting every remaining step down by 1 (StepMediat=1 ... StepEsikatselu=5).
Output: New SQL migration file + tightened bounds check in save-step/route.ts, with the
schema push applied to the live database.
</objective>
